<h2 id="biography">Biography</h2> 
Dr. Jin completed his postdoctoral study at the University of Oxford, UK, in 2015 and subsequently conducted cutting-edge research at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) and Bangor University. He has led multiple national and provincial/ministerial research projects with a total fund of >10m RMB. He has authored and/or co-authored more than 100 academic papers, one book under a national key publishing program, and has submitted two technical reports on next-generation mobile communications. He also holds multiple granted international and Chinese invention patents. He is deputy director of Dongguan Key Laboratory of Integrated Communication, Sensing and Computing Technologies for Swarm Intelligence ([link](https://www.gbu.edu.cn/sbj_detail_g7yU_64/272.html)).
[link](https://www.gbu.edu.cn/sbj_detail_g7yU_64/272.html)


<h2 id="research">Research Interests</h2> 
His research interests include high-capacity intelligent optical communication and signal processing, free-space optical transmission and positioning, light source tracking, optical fiber waveguides, and optical access networks, which have potential applications such as satellite internet, robotics/unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), low-altitude economy, and air-space-ground-sea integrated networks.
